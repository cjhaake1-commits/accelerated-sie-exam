# Accelerated SIE — Clean-Room Content Policy

## Purpose
This repository is being developed as an independently authored SIE preparation product. The commercial product must not reproduce, adapt, paraphrase too closely, or depend operationally on proprietary paid study manuals, question banks, lectures, flashcards, explanations, chapter sequencing, proprietary mnemonics, or other vendor-created expression.

## Controlling commercial sources
Commercial content may be based on facts, rules, concepts, calculations, and examination objectives obtained from public or independently licensed sources, including:

1. FINRA's current public SIE Content Outline and official public SIE practice-test materials.
2. Public FINRA rules, notices, investor-education pages, registration/qualification pages, and BrokerCheck/CRD descriptions.
3. Public SEC statutes, rules, investor bulletins, EDGAR materials, and educational resources.
4. Public MSRB/EMMA materials for municipal-securities concepts.
5. Public SIPC materials for customer-protection concepts.
6. Public Treasury/Federal Reserve/IRS materials where applicable to government securities, economics, retirement/tax concepts, and other tested facts.
7. Independently authored examples, explanations, calculations, scenarios, mnemonics, diagrams, and questions created specifically for this product.

## Excluded sources for commercial authoring
Paid course materials, licensed PDFs, proprietary question banks, recorded classes, private instructor notes, and vendor-only online content may be used by an individual purchaser for that purchaser's personal study only. They are NOT approved source material for the commercial question bank, commercial lessons, commercial flashcards, product architecture, or marketing claims.

No production item should cite a paid vendor manual as its source basis. No paid-vendor chapter number, distinctive wording, proprietary explanation, proprietary question stem, proprietary distractor set, or vendor-specific sequence should be required for the commercial product to function.

## Clean-room rewrite rule
Before paid launch, all lesson text, flashcards, equations, examples, and questions that were initially drafted while a proprietary study manual was available must be re-reviewed against public-source objectives and either:

- independently re-authored from the public source record, or
- removed from the commercial build.

The fact that a securities concept is common knowledge does not make a vendor's particular wording, selection, ordering, explanation, example, or question design free to copy.

## Question-bank rule
Questions must be original. Public FINRA practice questions may be used to calibrate format, difficulty, ambiguity control, and distractor quality, but must not be copied or lightly rewritten. Each production question should have:

- a stable item ID,
- a public-source objective or rule basis,
- an independently authored stem and choices,
- rationale for the correct answer,
- rationale for each distractor,
- difficulty and style metadata,
- review date/status,
- a clean-room attestation field.

## Founder beta / first test subject
The initial founder beta is a real-world validation cohort of one. Founder performance can be used to improve UX, pacing, explanations, remediation, question difficulty, and readiness-model calibration. It must not be marketed as a statistically validated pass-rate claim.

Track at minimum:

- study minutes,
- objective mastery before/after,
- practice scores on unseen items,
- confidence calibration,
- due-review completion,
- full simulated-exam scores and timing,
- date/result of the real SIE attempt if voluntarily entered.

## Marketing guardrails
Until statistically meaningful external validation exists, do not claim:

- a guaranteed pass,
- a validated probability of passing,
- FINRA affiliation or endorsement,
- that questions are actual/live FINRA questions,
- superiority over a named competitor without substantiated evidence.

Permissible positioning can describe independently authored preparation, blueprint alignment, adaptive practice, spaced review, diagnostic readiness, and founder-beta results as an individual case study.

## Legal review
This policy is a product-development safeguard, not legal advice. Before paid distribution, obtain qualified intellectual-property counsel review of the production content, source registry, branding, terms, privacy policy, disclaimers, and any claims involving exam outcomes or competitors.
